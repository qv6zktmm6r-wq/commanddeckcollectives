# Command Deck Collective LLC

Complete website source and assets for the approved black-and-gold Command Deck Collective storefront.

## Current preview

https://command-deck-collectives.mejia1604.chatgpt.site

The Sites preview is private to its owner. This repository contains the complete published build, including its source, images and hosting manifest.

## Included

- Approved photographic hero and the original crest logo, upscaled to HD (see "Logo" below).
- Six-link navigation: Shop, Services, Our Work, How it works, About, Custom Orders. Search, account and cart sit in the header icons and the mobile menu.
- Five populated category blocks: real Shopify coin photograph plus apparel, awards, bulk-order and custom-project concepts.
- Three featured products. In Shopify they are "Featured product" blocks that show the live photo, title and price; the bundled photos are a fallback.
- Instagram logo links to @commanddeckllc in the desktop header, mobile menu and footer; the Shopify footer also lists the store's policy pages.
- Custom-order form with name, email, organization, project type, quantity, needed-by date, budget range and project details.
- "Our Work" page (`preview/our-work.html`; Shopify template `page.our-work`): a photo gallery the owner fills by uploading pictures in the theme editor. Photos are never cropped and open larger in a photo viewer. It shows the three store coins until photos are added.
- Shopify-only "Recent work" homepage gallery and testimonial blocks that stay hidden until real photos or quotes are added.
- Owner phone app (`preview/owner.html`, "CD Owner"): an installable Home Screen app with the crest icon. It opens big shortcuts to Shopify orders, products, stats, customers, discounts and the theme editor (including the Our Work photos), Gmail for custom-order request emails, the live store and Instagram, plus the custom-order board (see below). The shortcuts store no passwords or customer data; each service asks for its own sign-in. It is hosted with the static site in `dist/` (not in the Shopify theme) on the Vercel project `command-deck-owner`: https://command-deck-owner.vercel.app opens the app (search engines are told not to index it). Publish updates with `./scripts/deploy-owner-app.sh`. Planned: move it to a custom subdomain once DNS is set up; the phone app must then be re-added from the new address.
- Social share image (`assets/command-deck-share.jpg`, 1200×630) and favicon/home-screen icons cut from the crest.
- Responsive styles, navigation and the custom-project form layout.
- A complete Shopify theme built on Dawn, plus installation instructions.
- Static deployment output in `dist/` and source preview in `preview/`.

## Project layout

- `assets/`: Command Deck styles (`command-deck.css`), JavaScript (`command-deck.js`), logo, product photos and concept imagery (WebP, sized for display), alongside Dawn's theme assets.
- `preview/index.html`, `preview/our-work.html`: standalone page sources.
- `preview/owner.html`, `owner.js`, `owner.webmanifest`, `owner-sw.js` with `assets/owner.css` and `assets/owner-icon-*.png`: the owner phone app. `preview/board.html`, `board-config.js`, `supabase-2.117.2.js` with `assets/owner-board*.{js,css}` and `assets/owner-stock.js`: the custom-order board and its Stock tab. These are excluded from the Shopify theme zip and `.shopifyignore`.
- `supabase/migrations/`: database schema for the board, applied in filename order; `supabase/tests/*_rls.sql`: access-rule checks; `supabase/functions/shopify-stock/`: the Stock tab's Shopify connection.
- `tests/`: `npm test` runs the Liquid section render checks, the board's email-reader tests and the stock function tests.
- `dist/`: static Site deployment output, generated by `scripts/build-dist.sh` (copies only the assets the pages use) — do not edit by hand.
- `layout/`, `sections/`, `snippets/`, `templates/`, `config/`, `locales/`: Shopify theme source, based on [Dawn](https://github.com/Shopify/dawn) (commit `258f00f`, MIT, see `LICENSE-DAWN.md`). Dawn supplies the product, collection, cart, search, account, blog and policy pages; the "Store pages" rules in `command-deck.css` give them the homepage look (serif titles, gold prices and buttons, bordered product cards), and `sections/main-404.liquid` adds a Custom Project link. Command Deck files are prefixed `cd-` or named `command-deck-*` / `custom-project`: the header and footer are `sections/cd-header.liquid` and `sections/cd-footer.liquid` (loaded through `header-group.json` / `footer-group.json`), the homepage is `sections/command-deck-home.liquid`, the Our Work page is `sections/cd-our-work.liquid` (photo tiles and viewer in `snippets/cd-gallery-item.liquid` / `cd-lightbox.liquid`), and both custom-order forms share `snippets/cd-contact-form.liquid`.
- `.openai/hosting.json`: existing Sites identity and static output configuration.
- `docs/`: visual specifications and asset/source provenance.

To view locally, run `python3 -m http.server 8000` from the repository root and open `http://localhost:8000/preview/`. No dependency installation is required.

After changing the standalone page or assets, run `./scripts/build-dist.sh` before deploying Sites. Shopify files use Shopify asset URLs and contact-form handling; validate them with `npx @shopify/cli theme check --path .`.

## Custom-order board

`board.html` in the owner app tracks custom-order requests from first email to shipped: New, Quoting, Design, Approved, In production, Shipped (and Archived). Each card shows the due date, quote or budget and how long it has sat in its stage; the summary boxes count new requests, work in progress, orders due this week, overdue orders and the value of approved work. "Paste email" reads a website request email and fills in a new card.

- Data lives in the Supabase project `command-deck-owner` (ref `tfavgqqqagcqhfeeutzc`, organization "Command Deck Collective", free plan, us-west-2). The page talks to it directly with the public publishable key in `preview/board-config.js`; the database's row-level security is what keeps orders private: only accounts listed in `public.owners` can read or change them, sign-ups are disabled and passwords need at least 12 characters.
- Add an owner (anyone added sees all orders, customer details, stock and sales):
  1. Supabase dashboard → Authentication → Users → Add user → Create new user: their email, a temporary password of at least 12 characters, and "Auto Confirm User" on.
  2. SQL editor: `insert into public.owners (user_id) select id from auth.users where email = '<their email>';`
  3. Give them the temporary password in person (not by text or email). On their phone they open the owner app's board, sign in, and tap **Change password** at the bottom of the board; it asks for the current password first.
  4. On an iPhone, open the owner app in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  To remove someone: `delete from public.owners where user_id = (select id from auth.users where email = '<their email>');` and delete the user under Authentication → Users. "Forgot password?" emails only arrive once Supabase can send email to that address (custom SMTP).
- Requests can only be deleted after they are moved to Archived, so a mis-tap can't remove active work.
- Free Supabase projects pause after about a week without use; opening the dashboard restores them. Supabase does not offer restorable backups on the free plan, so export important orders (Table editor → Export to CSV) now and then.
- Schema changes go in a new file in `supabase/migrations/` (never edit an applied one). After applying, run `supabase/tests/custom_orders_rls.sql` in the SQL editor: it rolls itself back and prints PASS/FAIL lines.
- The vendored `preview/supabase-2.117.2.js` must stay byte-identical to the pinned npm package; CI checks this with `npm run check-vendor`.

## Stock & bulk sales

The board's second tab (`board.html#stock`, also the "Stock & bulk sales" button in the owner app) shows Shopify stock by product and SKU, how many full boxes are left, low and out-of-stock items, and lets the owner record in-person or invoiced sales. Website orders already subtract stock in Shopify on their own.

- **Boxes are Shopify multipacks.** Stock is counted in single items (one SKU, e.g. `CDC-COIN-AAV`). A box is made with Shopify's free Bundles app as a multipack of that item (its own SKU and price, e.g. `CDC-COIN-AAV-BX25`), so selling one box takes 25 off the single-item count. Don't list a box as a separate product with its own stock number; that count never moves when singles sell.
- **Recording a sale** creates a real Shopify order (tagged `owner-app`, `in-person`), marked paid or "payment pending", so stock, sales reports and Shopify's order list stay in one place. The customer's email is never sent to Shopify, so Shopify has no address to send order emails to. Each sale is also logged in the Supabase table `stock_sales`; a repeated tap or retry with the same request id never records a second order.
- **How it connects.** `supabase/functions/shopify-stock/` is a Supabase Edge Function that holds the Shopify credentials; the browser only sends the owner's sign-in, and the function refuses anyone not in `public.owners`. It uses Shopify's Admin GraphQL API version `2026-07` (supported until about mid-2027; `draftOrderComplete`'s `paymentPending` argument is deprecated and should move to payment terms when the version is raised).
- **To connect (needs Shopify access, owner-gated):**
  1. In the Shopify Dev Dashboard, in the same organization as the store, create an app with the scopes `read_products`, `read_inventory`, `write_draft_orders` and `read_orders`, release a version and install it on the store.
  2. Store the app's client ID and secret as Supabase function secrets (never in this repository): `supabase secrets set --project-ref tfavgqqqagcqhfeeutzc SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN=fs8r63-zc.myshopify.com SHOPIFY_CLIENT_ID=… SHOPIFY_CLIENT_SECRET=…`. Optional `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (comma-separated) defaults to `https://command-deck-owner.vercel.app`.
  3. Deploy: `supabase functions deploy shopify-stock --no-verify-jwt --project-ref tfavgqqqagcqhfeeutzc` (the function checks the sign-in itself).
  Until then the tab says Shopify isn't connected.
- Tests: `tests/stock-function.mjs` (part of `npm test`) runs the function against simulated Supabase and Shopify responses; `supabase/tests/stock_sales_rls.sql` checks the sales log's access rules.

## Logo

`assets/command-deck-logo.webp` (1036×920) and `command-deck-logo-518.webp` are 4× upscales of the original 259×230 crest, made with Real-ESRGAN and then color-matched back to the original so the artwork, colors and wording are unchanged. Downscaled back to 259×230 the HD file matches the original at about 32 dB PSNR. The wordmark line was then changed from "COMMAND DECK COLLECTIVE, LLC" to "COMMAND DECK COLLECTIVE" by removing ", LLC" and re-centering the original lettering under the crest (no letters were redrawn). The original is kept in `docs/brand/command-deck-logo-original-259.webp`, with a lossless HD master in `docs/brand/command-deck-logo-hd-1036.png`. `docs/brand/command-deck-logo-transparent-1036.png` (and `.webp`) is a transparent-background version for light backgrounds, merch and print; its pale wordmark has low contrast on white or gold. `docs/brand/share-card.html` is the source for the share image.

The coin mockup in `assets/hero-workshop.webp` and `assets/approved-product-reference.webp` had the same rim text. Its ", LLC" was removed the same way: the remaining original lettering was shifted along the rim to stay centered under the laurel. Only the rim lettering changed; the rest of each image is untouched apart from re-encoding.

Smaller copies used for phones and small slots: `*-480.webp` (category art), `*-coin-500.webp` (coin photos) and `command-deck-logo-160.webp` (header).
