
# Approved Visual Direction

## Brand
Command Deck Collective LLC

Use the owner-provided black-and-gold crest logo as the primary brand mark. Do not redesign or replace it.

## Visual direction
Premium commercial presentation with a restrained military heritage influence. The homepage should feel credible to a business customer while still recognizing Command Deck's veteran and unit roots.

### Core palette
- Near-black background
- Warm metallic gold accents
- Cream / white typography
- Dark bronze and leather-like supporting tones

### Approved hero copy
CUSTOM PRODUCTS. MEANINGFUL IMPACT. BUILT TO LAST.

YOUR STORY
IN EVERY DETAIL.

Custom challenge coins, apparel, awards, engraved products, and premium merchandise for units, businesses, organizations, and individuals — designed to inspire, celebrate and leave a lasting legacy.

Primary CTA: START A CUSTOM PROJECT
Secondary CTA: SHOP COLLECTIONS

## Homepage categories
1. Challenge Coins
2. Apparel & Merchandise
3. Awards & Recognition
4. Bulk Orders
5. Custom Projects

Photography and Government categories were intentionally removed from the approved direction.

## Trust row
- Veteran Owned — Service Never Stops
- Quality Craftsmanship — Built to Last
- Custom Designs — Bring Your Vision to Life
- Bulk Orders Welcome — Teams · Businesses · Individuals
- Fast Fulfillment — Ready When You Are

## Implementation rule
Shopify remains the commerce engine. The redesign should improve brand presentation, custom-order lead capture, navigation, and conversion without replacing Shopify checkout, products, customer accounts, or fulfillment.
