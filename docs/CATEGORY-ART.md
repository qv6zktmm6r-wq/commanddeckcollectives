# Category imagery

Challenge Coins uses the original LVT-2 Water Buffalo product photograph from the Shopify store. The four other categories use illustrative concepts generated with the built-in image tool and the actual logo reference. They are labeled Design concept.

Assets: assets/category-apparel.webp; assets/category-awards.webp; assets/category-bulk.webp; assets/category-custom.webp.

Prompt set: premium wide black-and-gold product photographs with warm amber lighting and the supplied crowned CD laurel crest. Apparel: black hoodie, black cap, folded olive shirt. Awards: walnut recognition plaque and crystal trophy. Bulk: gift boxes, tumbler, pen and medallion. Custom: crest drawing, pen, medallion and calipers on a dark workbench. No webpage or category text in images.

Replaced the earlier offscreen screenshot crop technique with individual image elements and object-fit styling. Category images load eagerly. Desktop uses five columns; mobile uses two columns plus a full-width fifth card. Browser visual QA is unavailable in this environment; file decoding, paths, markup, and removal of crop offsets were verified.
